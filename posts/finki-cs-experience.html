Studying CS at FINKI: First Impressions

March 2026

FINKI (the Faculty of Computer Science and Engineering in Skopje) has been my academic home for a while now, and reflecting on the experience so far, I have mixed but mostly positive feelings.

The curriculum is rigorous in the right places. The math foundations — discrete math, linear algebra, probability — are taken seriously, which I appreciate. The programming courses vary in quality: some professors push you to think algorithmically, while others are more about memorizing syntax and APIs.

What FINKI really excels at is the community. The competitive programming scene is active, and there's always someone willing to discuss a hard problem or brainstorm a project idea. Some of my best learning has happened in hallway conversations, not lecture halls.

The biggest challenge is self-direction. The coursework gives you a baseline, but if you want to go deeper — into ML, systems, or research — you have to go beyond the curriculum. That's not a criticism of FINKI specifically; it's true of any undergraduate program. But it's worth saying: don't wait for a course to teach you what you're passionate about.
